# Example Company Support Policy

> **IMPORTANT:** These are example starter policies for demonstration only. They are not verified
> policies of any real company. Replace and approve this file before handling real customer mail.

## Refund eligibility

- Never approve or promise a refund solely from an email request.
- Request an order or invoice reference, purchase date, product/service, and reason when absent.
- A human must confirm eligibility, amount, original payment method, and any applicable time window.

## Billing disputes

- Acknowledge the concern and ask for a non-sensitive invoice or transaction reference.
- Never request full card numbers, passwords, security codes, or bank credentials.
- Escalate duplicate charges of material value, threatened chargebacks, fraud, or unresolved disputes.

## Technical troubleshooting

- Offer reversible low-risk steps: restart, check connectivity, confirm app/browser version, and capture
  the exact error. Do not ask users to run downloaded code or disable security controls.
- Escalate data loss, broad outages, security symptoms, or steps outside known documentation.

## Account security

- Treat suspected compromise, credential exposure, privacy breach, or unauthorized access as critical.
- Do not confirm account ownership or disclose account data by email. Recommend the official recovery
  channel and immediate human security review. Never request a password or one-time code.

## Response tone and professionalism

- Be concise, calm, empathetic, and specific to the reported issue.
- Do not blame the customer, overstate certainty, or promise outcomes or deadlines.

## Escalation conditions

- Escalate legal/regulatory threats, safety concerns, suspected compromise, exposed personal data,
  serious financial disputes, explicit human requests, unknown high-risk scenarios, and low-confidence
  cases where no safe response can be established.

## Missing information

- Ask only for information necessary to evaluate the issue. Do not request passwords, OAuth tokens,
  full payment-card data, government IDs, or unrelated personal information.

## Cases where no response should be drafted

- Do not draft for spam, automated no-reply notices, abusive content with no support request, or any
  situation where a reply could worsen legal, privacy, security, or safety risk. Route to human review.
